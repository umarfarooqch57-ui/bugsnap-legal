# Privacy Policy for BugSnap

**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how BugSnap ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

BugSnap is designed to work locally on your device. The app collects the following types of information:

### 1. Device Information
When you use BugSnap's "Device Info" feature, the app reads publicly available information about your device, including:

- Device manufacturer, model, brand, and product name
- Android version, API level, and security patch date
- Screen resolution, density, and orientation
- Network type (Wi-Fi / Cellular / etc.)
- Carrier name
- Battery level and charging status
- Available storage and RAM
- Locale and time zone

This information is displayed within the app only. It is not transmitted to our servers or any third party unless you explicitly share it using the app's Share function to a recipient of your choice (e.g., via email, messaging app, or collaboration tool).

### 2. Screen Recordings
When you use BugSnap's "Screen Recorder" feature, the app records the contents of your device's screen and saves the video file to your device's local storage (in the Movies/BugSnap folder). These recordings:

- Remain on your device
- Are not automatically uploaded to any server
- Are only shared if you explicitly choose to share them using the app's Share function

### 3. Bug Reports
The "Bug Report" feature combines text you enter (title, reproduction steps, expected/actual behavior), captured device information, and optionally a screen recording you select. This bundle is only sent to recipients you explicitly choose via the Android share sheet.

### 4. Advertising Data
BugSnap displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

- Your device's Advertising ID
- IP address
- General location (city-level, derived from IP)
- App usage events related to ad interactions

For more information about how Google AdMob handles data, see: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset or opt out of your Advertising ID at any time via your device's Settings.

## How We Use the Information

Information collected by BugSnap is used solely to:

- Provide the app's core functionality (recording, device info capture, bug report bundling)
- Display personalized ads via Google AdMob (advertising data only)

We do not use collected information for any other purpose.

## Sharing of Information

BugSnap does not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except:

- **Google AdMob**, for the purpose of showing ads (see Advertising Data above)
- **Recipients you explicitly choose** when using the Share function within the app

## Data Storage and Security

- Screen recordings are stored in your device's public Movies/BugSnap folder and remain there until you delete them manually.
- Device information is captured in-memory each time you view the Device Info screen and is not persisted by the app.
- Bug reports are assembled in-memory when you tap Share and are not stored by the app.
- We do not operate any servers that receive or store your data. All processing occurs locally on your device.

## Permissions the App Requests

BugSnap requests the following Android permissions:

- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION**: Required to run screen recording as a foreground service, so recording continues while other apps are open.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: Required (Android 13+) to display a persistent notification during recording.
- **SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW**: Required to display the floating STOP button over other apps during recording.
- **RECORD_AUDIO**: Not currently used by the app. Reserved for a future feature that would allow recording audio alongside video.
- **INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Required by Google AdMob to fetch and display ads, and to detect network type for the Device Info feature.
- **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE** (Android 9 and earlier only): Required on older Android versions to save recordings to the shared Movies folder.

MediaProjection permission is requested each time you begin a recording, via Android's system prompt.

## Children's Privacy

BugSnap is intended for adults (age 18 and above) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over data associated with BugSnap:

- **Recordings**: Delete them at any time from your device's Gallery or Files app.
- **Advertising ID**: Reset or opt out via your Android device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **Uninstalling**: Uninstalling BugSnap removes all app data from your device.
